How does SportsIntelAI predict matches?+

The agent builds a pre-match view of each team using Elo strength, recent form, goals scored and conceded, home/away performance, rest difference and match context.

A multiclass machine-learning model estimates Home, Draw and Away probabilities. Predictions remain provisional until 48 hours before kickoff. Once locked, the official prediction cannot be changed.
